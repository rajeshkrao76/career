About Raveone

MSME hiring partners connecting exceptional talent with leading manufacturers, engineering firms and enterprises across India.

Who we are

Raveone Consultants is an executive search and selection firm headquartered in Bangalore, serving manufacturers, engineering companies and enterprises across India. We specialise in plant-level technical roles, quality functions, and mid-to-senior management hiring.

As an MSME registered vendor, we partner with more than 100 organisations — from small family-owned engineering workshops to large multi-plant manufacturers — helping them find engineers, technicians, managers and specialists who understand their industry.

What we do

  • Permanent placement — plant engineers, quality engineers, maintenance, production, design
  • Executive search — mid-to-senior management roles in manufacturing and engineering
  • Bulk hiring — walk-in drives, campus hiring, contract-to-hire
  • Talent pipeline — sourcing, screening, assessment and onboarding support

Why candidates choose us

  • Verified, real job openings from registered employers — no fake postings
  • Direct with the hiring company — no middlemen, no fee
  • Transparent process — candidates can track their applications on the Raveone portal
  • Weekly job alerts matched to your skills and preferred locations

Why employers choose us

  • Deep manufacturing and engineering domain expertise
  • Pre-screened, verified candidate pool — no time wasted on unqualified CVs
  • Short turnaround — from requirement to shortlist in under 5 working days
  • MSME-friendly pricing, no retainers required for standard roles

Our Process

A transparent, tech-enabled hiring process — from requirement to onboarding.

For candidates

1

Apply to a job or submit your CV

Browse open roles and apply in 60 seconds — or submit your CV to our talent pool if you don't see the perfect fit yet.

2

Initial screening

Our recruiter reviews your profile and (for shortlisted candidates) calls you to understand your experience, expectations and availability.

3

Client submission

If you're a fit, we submit your profile to the hiring company with a cover note. You'll get a notification and can track the status on our portal.

4

Interview scheduling

The client reviews your profile and (if shortlisted) we schedule an interview — in person, phone or video. You'll receive calendar invites and reminders.

5

Offer and onboarding

If selected, we coordinate the offer, notice period and joining. We stay in touch through onboarding and beyond.

For employers

1

Share your requirement

Submit the role via our employer form — title, location, CTC range, experience, key skills and any specific requirements.

2

We align on the brief

A senior recruiter calls you to understand the role, team and culture — and agree on a target profile and timeline.

3

We source and screen

We shortlist 5–10 qualified profiles within 5 working days, all pre-screened for experience, CTC alignment and availability.

4

Interviews and feedback

We schedule interviews, share CVs, and collect structured feedback from both sides through the client portal.

5

Offer and closing

We assist with the offer, negotiate if needed, and follow up through notice and joining. Replacement guarantee included.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

1. What we collect

When you apply to a job or submit your CV, we collect:

  • Identity & contact — name, email, phone, location
  • Professional — resume, experience, skills, education, CTC expectations, notice period
  • Usage — IP address, browser type, pages visited (via server logs)
  • Portal — if you register, your login email and password hash

2. How we use it

  • To process your job application and communicate with you about it
  • To share your profile with the hiring company for the role you applied to
  • To match you with relevant future roles (if you opt into job alerts)
  • To comply with legal obligations

3. Who we share it with

We share candidate profile data only with the specific hiring company for the job you applied to. We do not sell your data to third parties, and we do not share it with advertisers.

If you apply to a role with Company X, your profile is visible to the relevant HR / hiring team at Company X. It is not visible to other clients, to the public, or to any third party.

4. Data storage & security

Your data is stored in Google Workspace (Sheets, Drive) with access restricted to authorised Raveone recruiters. Uploaded resumes are stored in a private Google Drive folder accessible only through the ATS.

We use industry-standard security measures, but no system is 100% secure. If you suspect unauthorised access to your data, contact us immediately at rajesh@raveone.in.

5. Data retention

Candidate profiles are retained for up to 12 months from the date of submission, unless you request deletion earlier. After this period, profiles are deleted unless you re-apply or opt into longer-term talent pool storage.

6. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • Access a copy of the data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data (except where retention is legally required)
  • Withdraw consent for job alerts at any time

To exercise any of these rights, email rajesh@raveone.in with the subject "Data Request".

7. Cookies

The Site uses minimal cookies — strictly for session management (if you log in) and to remember your preferences. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

8. Contact

For any privacy questions, email rajesh@raveone.in.
